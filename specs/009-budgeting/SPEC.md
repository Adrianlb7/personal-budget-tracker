# 009 Budgeting Spec

## Goal

Support monthly budgets by category and current month savings metrics.

## Scope

- Monthly category budget amounts.
- Current spend by category.
- Total budgeted vs spent.
- Monthly income, expenses, saved, and savings rate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Budgets are user-owned.
- Transfers and reimbursements do not count as spending.
- Zero-income savings rate displays as not applicable.
- Budget calculations are deterministic and tested.
