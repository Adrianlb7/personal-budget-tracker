# 012 Goals Spec

## Goal

Track financial goals without duplicating account balances.

## Scope

- Goal name.
- Target amount and date.
- Optional linked savings account.
- Progress and required monthly contribution.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Goals belong to users.
- Linked account balances are referenced, not duplicated as new assets.
- Progress calculations are deterministic.
- Required monthly contribution handles past or missing target dates.
