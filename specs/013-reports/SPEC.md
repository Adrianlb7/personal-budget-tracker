# 013 Reports Spec

## Goal

Provide useful financial reports without visual clutter.

## Scope

- Net worth over time.
- Monthly income vs expenses.
- Savings rate.
- Spending by category.
- Cash flow.
- Time filters: 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, ALL.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Reports answer clear financial questions.
- Transfer activity is excluded from income/expense charts.
- Snapshot and derived values are used consistently.
- FX assumptions are visible where relevant.
