# 011 Investments Spec

## Goal

Track simple investment holdings without becoming a trading platform.

## Scope

- Investment account/holding records.
- Contributions or cost basis.
- Current value.
- Gain and return.
- Liquid vs invested asset distinction.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Investments contribute to net worth.
- Liquid and invested assets are distinct in UI.
- Manual valuation is supported.
- Schema can support future price providers.
