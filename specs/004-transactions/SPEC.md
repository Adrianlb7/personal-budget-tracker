# 004 Transactions Spec

## Goal

Record income and expense transactions while preserving original currency and category context.

## Scope

- Income and expense transaction creation.
- Transaction list.
- Categories.
- Notes and metadata.
- Transaction date.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Income increases account balance and monthly income.
- Expense decreases account balance and monthly expenses.
- Original amount and currency are preserved.
- Categories are user-owned and extensible.
- Domain tests cover balance, income, expense, and precision behavior.
