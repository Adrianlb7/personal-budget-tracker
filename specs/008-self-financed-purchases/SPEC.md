# 008 Self-Financed Purchases Spec

## Goal

Track purchases paid from savings and future reimbursements without double-counting expenses.

## Scope

- Original expense transaction.
- Reimbursement plan.
- Installment count and progress.
- Linked transfer repayments.
- Projected savings after reimbursements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Original purchase is counted as the expense.
- Repayments are transfers only.
- Repayments are linked to the plan.
- Remaining reimbursement amount is accurate.
- Current balance and projected after-reimbursement balance are clearly distinct.
