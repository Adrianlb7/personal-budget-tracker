# Product

## Vision

Personal Finance Hub is a clean, modern dashboard for one person to understand:

- How much money they currently have
- Where money is located
- How money is moving
- Spending, income, savings, and savings rate
- Recurring financial commitments
- Upcoming payments
- Net worth over time

It is not a bank, exchange, accounting suite, trading platform, or enterprise finance system.

## Primary Navigation

- Dashboard
- Accounts
- Transactions
- Budget
- Recurring
- Investments
- Goals
- Reports
- Settings

The global `Add transaction` action should be easily reachable.

## MVP Boundaries

The first usable version should support authenticated personal finance tracking with accounts, transactions, transfers, basic dashboard metrics, monthly budgets, recurring commitments, and deterministic financial calculations.

Automatic bank sync, automatic FX rates, market-price integrations, advanced tax/accounting features, GraphQL, microservices, Redis, Kubernetes, and separate backend services are out of scope until a real need appears.
